The goal

  • Flatter, less bloated stomach; firmer, rounder glutes; a tighter back.
  • Eat enough to build, not to shrink: your weight should stay about the same. Weigh on days 1, 8 and 14.
  • Two weeks gives less bloating, a tighter core and firmer glutes. Visible glute growth takes 8–12 weeks.

How to train

  • Stop each set 1–2 reps before you can't do another, with clean form. That's what makes 3–5 kg work.
  • Tempo 3-1-1: 3 s down, 1 s pause, 1 s up. Squeeze the glutes 2 s at the top of thrusts and bridges.
  • Women recover fast between sets: keep rests short, as listed.
  • 4a + 4b is a superset: do 4a, go straight to 4b, then rest.
  • Sprints are truly all-out for 30 s, then 2 full minutes easy. Two days a week only.
